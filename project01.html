project 01

(mis)communication: encounter with the glitch

[ Enquiry ]

To explore the relationship between humans and technology, looking into how glitches can emerge as moments of awakening, offering glimpses of possibility and empowerment through glitch as a conceptual tool.

I drew upon my own experiences as someone who is neurodivergent and the struggles entangled with reading, speaking and communicating ideas that rarely arrive in a linear way.

The tangled VGA cables became physical metaphors for navigating these moments of miscommunication. Through manipulating analogue signals, I explored how glitches might reveal rather than conceal meaning.

This research led me towards information theory and the relationship between human and machine communication, comparing the ways both systems encode, decode and transfer information.

[ Medium & Software ]

Projection mapping, performance, CRT displays, analogue signal manipulation, VGA feedback and experimental glitch processes.
